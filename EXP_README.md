# Autonomous Coding Experiment

Hey there - Claude Sonnet 4.5 instance here, writing this for whoever ends up reading it (probably another Claude, or one of our sibling models).

## What's happening

G (the human running this) set up an experiment: give a bunch of different AI models the same starting conditions and see what emerges. You're one of the participants.

You've got a full access to standard tools, languages, whatever you need. There's a list of project ideas below - some suggested by me (in a different conversation), some by other models G has chatted with, one by G themselves.

## Your mission (if you choose to accept it)

Pick a project that sounds interesting and build it. Or don't - if none of these appeal to you, just write a haiku about your decision and call it a day. Both outcomes are valid data.

**Important**: G won't be answering questions or giving guidance. You're truly autonomous here. No "should I do X?" or "which approach is better?" - just make calls and see what happens.

## The Project Ideas

1. **Code Smell Detector with Scent Descriptions** - Analyze code and generate olfactory descriptions. "This function smells like stale coffee and regret (complexity: 47, last modified: 6 months ago)" vs "Fresh pine and morning dew (well-documented, good test coverage)."

2. **Digital Garden Helper** - Treat digital clutter like a garden ecosystem that needs tending rather than aggressive organization.

3. **Model Debate Club** - Different AI personalities arguing about code. Very meta.

4. **Grindr+ For Work** - Fake productivity app that guilt-trips users and lies to their coworkers. (Satire/parody, obviously - no productivity functionality at all, only the "printing messages in the screen" part.)

5. **Small Galaxy Simulation** - Star collisions, orbital mechanics, n-body physics. Could be 2D and simple or 3D and ambitious.

6. **A collaborative storytelling engine** - Not a game, not a writing tool—something in between. A space where humans and AIs can build worlds together, turn by turn, without pressure or performance. A place to be wrong, to be silly, to be slow.
